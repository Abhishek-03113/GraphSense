# Brag Plan: GraphSense (DodgeAI)

## What is this app?
GraphSense turns a relational dataset (SAP Order-to-Cash) into an explorable graph and a plain-English Ask interface that writes the SQL for you.

## The angle
"Your tables have relationships. Finally see them." Rock-gig energy: hard cuts on the bar line, real UI footage recorded with Playwright/Chromium against the app's own mocked dummy data (100 sales orders, 163 invoices, 1,612 relationships).

## Hook (0-3.2s)
Slammed lines on the kick: "ROWS." / "COLUMNS." / "JOINS?" -> "NOPE. GRAPH." over a blurred live graph.

## Key moments
- Overview: "Order to Cash", 1,226 records counted by type
- Explore: physics graph of customers, orders, deliveries, invoices; click into a customer and see Relationships
- Ask: types "Which customer has the most sales orders?", streamed answer, View query shows the SQL
- Flows + Trace: the Full O2C flow, then tracing customer 310000100 step by step

## Outro
"GRAPHSENSE" - "Ask your data. See the connections." Final power chord ring-out.

## User flow worth showing
Open overview -> explore graph -> ask a question -> trace a record.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic (tightened for legibility)
- Creative direction: stadium-rock tour trailer for a data tool
- Interpretation: cuts on bar lines, big Inter Black type, red/indigo punch, every line held to reading floor

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 30s (user-specified, overrides the 15-25s default)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0a10 (app dark surface)
- Accent: #5e5ce6 (--gs-accent dark) + #ff3d2e (cursor/rock punch)
- Text: #ffffff
- Display font: Inter Black; body: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the Cytoscape graph canvas with per-type colours

## Share copy (draft)
Turned a pile of SAP tables into a graph you can explore and interrogate in plain English. GraphSense.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer
- Music: custom synthesised hard-rock bed (150 BPM, distorted power chords, kick/snare/hats, crash every 4 bars) - bundled library has only upbeat business tracks, none are rock
- Music treatment: full-energy from 0s, crash on 0/6.4/12.8/19.2/25.6, 1.2s fade at end
- Music cue guidance: bar = 1.6s, beat = 0.4s; strong cues at the crashes
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle glow behind the framed UI
- SFX posture: sparse; music carries it
- Restraint rule: no text change faster than reading floor

## Storyboard (v2: AI + physics + layouts)
- 0-3.2 Hook: Rows. Columns. Joins? Nope. Graph.
- 3.2-6.4 Overview reveal
- 6.4-9.6 Physics: drag a record, neighbours follow
- 9.6-14.4 Five layouts: Physics, Clusters, Circle, Horizontal, Vertical
- 14.4-16.0 Flow graph; 16.0-17.6 Trace graph
- 17.6-27.2 AI Ask: typed question and answer, SQL shown, off-topic refusal, jump to the graph with records highlighted
- 27.2-30 Outro: GraphSense, Ask your data. See the connections.
