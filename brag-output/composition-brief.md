# Hyperframes Composition Brief: GraphSense

30s rock-trailer launch video for GraphSense.
## Output

Real footage recorded with Playwright + Chromium (dark mode, 1600x900) against the app's mocked API: `assets/footage/{overview,explore,ask,flows,trace,palette}.mp4`.
Verbatim copy: "Which customer has the most sales orders?", "Order to Cash", "1,226 records", "1,612 relationships".
## Creative Direction
Stadium-rock tour trailer; see brag-plan.md storyboard (the contract). Avoid generic SaaS language.
## Visual Identity
bg #0a0a10, text #fff, accents #5e5ce6 and #ff3d2e, Inter Black / Inter.
## Audio
Music: `assets/music/rock-riff.mp3` (150 BPM; crashes at 0, 6.4, 12.8, 19.2, 25.6). Scene cuts lock to those cues. Subtle audio-reactive glow. No separate SFX.
